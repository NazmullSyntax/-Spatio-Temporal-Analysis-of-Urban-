## 1.4 Research Questions

This study addresses the following research questions:

**RQ1: Historical Green Space Change (2000–2025)**
How has urban green space in Dhaka District changed spatially and 
temporally between 2000 and 2025, and what is the rate and pattern 
of this change?

**RQ2: Green Space and Land Surface Temperature**
What is the statistical and spatial relationship between green space 
loss and land surface temperature in Dhaka, both in daytime and 
nighttime conditions?

**RQ3: Population Exposure to Urban Heat**
Which populations and areas in Dhaka are most exposed to elevated 
urban heat, and how has this exposure changed over the study period?

**RQ4: Health and Economic Associations**
What associations can be identified between environmental exposure 
and available health and economic indicators?

**RQ5: Future Scenario for 2030**
Based on historical trends, what green space and heat conditions 
are projected for Dhaka in 2030?
