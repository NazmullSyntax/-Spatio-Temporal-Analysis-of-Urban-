# Day 9 — Impact Estimation Summary

### Exposed Population
- Above 30°C: 352,336 people (1.4% of Dhaka)
- Change from 2025: +118,765 (+51%)

| Heat-related hospitalizations | 117.6 | 177.4 | +59.8 |
| Heat-related ED visits | 483.7 | 728.6 | +244.9 |

**Attributable to heat above 30°C (2030):**
- 18 deaths
- 177 hospitalizations
- 729 ED visits

## Methodology Note

All estimates use published exposure-response coefficients:
- Heat-mortality: Gasparrini et al. (2015)
- Heat-hospitalization: Burkart et al. (2011)
- Productivity: ILO (2019)
- Electricity: IEA (2020)

These are ESTIMATES, not measured outcomes. Actual impacts may vary.

## Limitations

1. Ward-level health data not available — estimates based on national rates
2. Economic coefficients from global literature — may not perfectly fit Dhaka
3. Assumes linear exposure-response relationships
4. Does not account for adaptation measures
5. Value of Statistical Life (VSL) is approximate
